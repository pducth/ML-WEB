Behind the brand

How it started

FOLMARÉ joins folium, the leaf, and mare, the sea, for botanical calm, fluidity and a curated care journey. It began with a simple frustration: great haircare is scattered across brands, and it is hard to know which formula is truly right for your hair. So we built one calm place to shop the best of it.

The curated edit

We are a retailer, not a manufacturer. Four houses shape our shelf: AROMATICA for aromatic scalp rituals, Rahua for Amazonian nourishment, Davines for conscious professional care and Moroccanoil for argan glow and finishing. Every one is chosen for its point of view and its results.

FOLMARÉ today

What began as a small, hand-picked collection has grown into a full botanical haircare edit: shampoos, conditioners, masks, treatment oils, dry shampoos and styling finishers, all genuine, all guided by a Hair Quiz that builds your routine.

Haircare, made personal

Not sure where to begin? Our Hair Quiz turns salon know-how into a personal 4-step routine of cleanse, condition, treat and finish, matched to your hair type, concerns and goals. Your answers stay private: we only record anonymous statistics.
