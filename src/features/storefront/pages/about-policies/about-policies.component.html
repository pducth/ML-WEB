Our beginning

A new era for hair care

Moroccanoil began with a transformative experience: founder Carmen Tal discovered the remarkable effects of argan oil after a visit to a salon. Inspired by the way it helped restore the look and feel of her hair, she set out to share this discovery with the world.

That first encounter became a movement rooted in performance, sensorial beauty, and a deep appreciation for the traditions behind argan oil.
