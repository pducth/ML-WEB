# FOLMARÉ — Brand Identity Guidelines

**Name concept:** *Folium* (leaf) + *Mare* (sea). An invented retail brand name suggesting botanical calm, fluidity and a curated care journey.

**Position:** Botanical-inspired premium multi-brand HairCare retail, not a cosmetics manufacturer.

**Brand line:** Nature, Felt in Every Ritual.  
**Descriptor:** BOTANICAL HAIRCARE EDIT.

**Four curatorial pillars**

| Brand | Story role | Suggested department |
|---|---|---|
| AROMATICA | Roots / Aromatic scalp rituals | Scalp Care, Shampoo, Serum |
| Rahua | Botanical nourishment / Amazon | Shampoo, Conditioner, Mask, Oil |
| Davines | Conscious professional care | Mask, Treatment, Conditioner |
| Moroccanoil | Argan glow, hair finishing | Oil, Styling, Treatment |

**Color ratio:** mostly Cream and Pearl Mist (70%), Forest and Sage (20%), Coral gradient details (10%). Reserve forest for text and buttons. Do not rely on coral for tiny white text. Respect color contrast.

**Logo**
- Primary vertical: website About, packaging graphic mockups, social brand profile.
- Wordmark only: main navbar.
- F monogram: favicon, compact badges.
- White: only against Forest or deep-gradient sections.
- Clear space around logo: at least one height of its letter F crossbar on each side.
- Do not stretch, skew, crop the F's hairline or redraw the star separately.
- Prefer PNG for exact appearance matching the approved picture; choose traced SVG if flexible scaling is needed.

**Photography:** creamy botanical textures, translucent oil, flowing leaves, sage/sea-mist glass, brushed metallic botanicals. Visual assets in reference images are design studies, not verified catalog product photography.

**Texture-video cards:** when you legally have the video for a product, use HTML `<video muted playsinline loop preload="none" poster="...">` on hover. Include controls or an explicit play button on touch and honor reduced-motion preferences. Fallback to a static product texture photo when media is missing.

**Typography:** Montserrat for UI headings and navigation; Lora for expressive editorial headlines. Both fonts loaded via Google Fonts on the demo; no font files are distributed.
